All lengths below are in metres. The first companion enters at room 3 and carries a conservative personal manual. The training stroke in each room supplies observed evidence; the live stroke adds a spatial interlock. The exit conditions bind to live geometry and state. Closed interlocks reject incompatible operations. Room 4 also has a reversible overpressure hazard: a second feed raises a red piston next to the receiving station. ## Live interlock separation

The selector, yoke and sight checks use the leader's station, while the opposite handle must be within interaction reach. The first live stroke starts from the reset handle position. The shortest seeded station-to-handle distance exceeds the sum of the positioning radius and the handle reach in rooms 3, 4 and 6 (approximately 4.020 m vs 3.900 m; 3.826 m vs 3.700 m; 3.880 m vs 3.760 m). In room 5 the 0.650 m plate radius plus the capstan's 1.450 m station radius fit inside a separation of over 3.4 m. The live stroke therefore needs a second operator. The shutter's masonry returns close off both outer sides, and the witness walks beneath the reopened centre leaf before taking the door.
