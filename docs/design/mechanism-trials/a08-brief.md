# A08 / The Tempered Span

The same bridge must hold sufficient cable tension and keep its moving shoe in the guide throughout a loaded crossing. Its service punch gets the proof pin out of the way so copper can be formed; the absent pin then blocks proof. At the Splice Bench, reset the pin, temperature, counterweight and brake to a visible earlier mark. The cable ratchet, copper bend and exterior drum keep their new state. Use the saved setup and both live needles to choose a rider approach and entry.

The eight numbered stations are connected by two diamonds. Use `1` Bench, `2` Jack, `3` Kiln, `4` Yoke, `5` Brake, `6` Weight and `7` Span. At the respective stations hold `W`/`S` to adjust; the action line describes the current station. At the Kiln `P` starts the service punch. At the Bench `M` marks, `H` restores Home and `B` restores Bench. `A` arms at the Yoke, `E` queues a certified rider window at an approach, and `X` withdraws or returns the rider. Space toggles live time; `.` advances one tick and `Tab` changes view without spending time. The proof ruler shows certified entry instants under the actual rider load, not the unloaded reading.
