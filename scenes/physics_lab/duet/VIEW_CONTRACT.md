# A06 renderer contract — The Felt Drawleaf

Preload `res://scenes/physics_lab/duet/DuetRules.gd` and instantiate with `.new()`. This room is one deterministic layout. Call `reset()` for a fresh run. The model owns every contact, sight lock, warning, crossing and result. No camera ray, audio-completion signal, animation or engine collider changes a decision.

## Driver

- `layout()` -> detached static dictionary. Plan coordinates are `[x,z]` in integer centimetres, +Z from porch to exit. `stations_cm` lists the walking marks; `gap_banks` are station indices. `ear_cm`, `watcher_rail_z_cm` (inclusive endpoints), `watcher_x_cm`, `watcher_eye_height_cm`, `parapet_x_cm`, `parapet_top_cm`, `leaf_hinge_cm`, `leaf_width_cm`, and `placards` are authored in the model. The low parapet runs alongside the crossing; the raised eye sees across it, the low eye does not. The two gap banks are iron, the four open spans between them are felt. The leaf pivots from the near bank (`angle_deg = 0` flat, `90` upright). The 20-tick stride and the 60 Hz model clock are fixed.
- `snapshot()` -> detached live dictionary (below). A fresh snapshot at `tick = 0` accepts a decision. At every tick divisible by 20, call `submit(action)` once at most; skipped input means `wait`. Due events are already processed at that tick before a new input. `submit` returns `{accepted, reason}`; a rejected command on an open boundary spends that slot and retains the previous posture. Subsequent submissions on that tick and off-boundary submissions return `decision_closed`. Read the submit-time event pulse before advancing.
- `tick_once()` advances one 60 Hz tick and replaces its `events` pulse. Render from the latest snapshots; read each tick if playing transient sounds. `step(action = "wait")` is the replay equivalent: submit and advance 20 ticks, returning `{accepted, reason, events, snapshot}`. Its events contain only new submit events and the ensuing ticks. A closed decision advances zero ticks. If polling both API calls on one tick, deduplicate events with their `id`.

| Action | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `lower` | Throw the lever from the safe porch when the leaf is upright and idle. |
| `watch` / `creep` | Move one mark toward the exit, tall with gaze held / low behind the parapet. |
| `back_watch` / `back_creep` | Move one mark toward the porch, using the same respective postures. |
| `wait` | Stay put and keep the current posture. Upright bracing still rings the spring-loaded iron tread; crouched waiting still leaves the watcher free. The covered porch is safe. |

Rejection IDs: `decision_closed`, `unknown_action`, `lever_out_of_reach`, `drive_engaged`, `end_of_lane`, `gap_open` (unsupported span), `recovering`, `won`. A recovering or winning player only accepts `wait`.

## Snapshot fields

- Top level: `tick`, `tick_hz`, `next_decision_tick`, `outcome` (`active`, `recovering`, `won`), `attempt` (starts at 1, increments on recovery), `player`, `leaf`, `hearing`, `sight`, `cues`, `events`.
- `player`: `position_cm`, `station` (last completed mark), `target_station`, `zone` (`porch`, `lane`, `exit`, `service`), `posture` (`tall`, `low`), `eye_height_cm`, `moving`, `footing` (`cover`, `iron`, `felt`, `service`), `on_leaf`. `position_cm` moves by 3 cm/tick on a normal stride. Use the service zone to show a separate underfloor return to the porch, not a walk across unsupported floor. The sensor fields describe the last completed tick: at submit the new posture is reserved, and at a bank arrival the last motion sample can still be felt.
- `leaf`: `phase` (`up`, `lower_warning`, `lowering`, `down`, `raise_warning`, `held`, `raising`), `angle_deg`, `passable`, `hold` (true during `held`). Lowering only makes the gap walkable at the fully down detent. A lift countdown may expire while the leaf is occupied; the pawl holds until the feet clear. `passable` is the actual gap topology.
- `hearing`: `ring`, `trip_ring`, `cap_ring`, `signal_per_tick` (signed contribution before clamping), `surface` (the last sampled material), `mode` (`idle`, `listening`, `warning`). The Listener is a fixed construct at `ear_cm`; propagate iron and felt foot sounds differently and show the resonator winding/unwinding even when an actor braces in place. `signal_per_tick` belongs to the authority; volume and timbre belong to the view.
- `sight`: `position_cm`, `separation_cm` (signed along the rail, player minus watcher), `reach_cm`, `gaze` (true means frozen), `mode` (`idle`, `frozen`, `tracking`, `winding`, `primed`). `primed` is a close-range wind-up that already matured while the watcher stayed frozen. Keep the live cue visible while frozen; clearing reach visibly releases it. At the reach limit the watcher can lunge across the rail; the Listener instead discharges its loaded resonator into the tread. Either catch sends the player under the crossing to the porch. The watcher runs along the side rail only when the player's low eye is screened.
- `cues`: active `{id, kind, start_tick, due_tick}`. Kinds: `leaf_lower` (steam and descending leaf until the down detent), `leaf_raise` (rising counterweight and visible pawl through any hold), `hearing` (rattling ear spring), `sight` (ratcheting limbs; show the held charge even while frozen), `recovery` (service return). Show distinct cues and real progress until `due_tick`; `held` and `primed` retain their cue at 100%.
- `events`: pulse of `{id, tick, kind, source, cue_id, start_tick, due_tick, lead_ticks, detail}`. Monotonic `id` is unique until `reset()`. `cue_id`/timing fields are `-1` for ordinary pulses. `cue_start`, `cue_cancel`, `leaf_passable`, `leaf_blocked`, `caught`, `recovered` reference the relevant cue. Ordinary pulses: `stride` (detail: `from`, `to`, `posture`, `surface`), `leaf_motion`, `leaf_hold`, `leaf_up`, `gaze_lock`, `gaze_lost`, `won`. `caught.source` identifies `hearing` or `sight`; `caught.detail.also` lists any simultaneous secondary cause. Cue kinds and event sources are string IDs. This is English-facing fixture data; localize only at the visual layer if desired.

Read the two gauges together: bracing on bare iron buys space from a stopped watcher and loads the resonator; creeping drains the resonator while the watcher closes the gap. The same tall stride on the lowered felt leaf can drain the resonator and hold the watcher in place. Moving, cue counters, the pawl, and recovery all stand on the same clock.
