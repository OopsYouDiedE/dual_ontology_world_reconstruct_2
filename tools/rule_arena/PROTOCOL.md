# Night Watch / contestant packet v1

Your role is to **write** one autonomous JavaScript program in 40 minutes. The referee runs it after the authoring clock ends. Deliver `submission.js` (UTF-8, at most 128 KiB, atomic saves). Only the author packet is available. No post-freeze tuning, manual play, model calls, referee code, reference bot, seeds or replay files. A commitment identifies the preselected evaluation cohort. A successful run reaches Main Camp Hall with the crew by tick 36,000.

## ABI and budget

Declare `function decide(o) { return integer; }`. QuickJS guest, synchronous ECMAScript; keep memory between steps (16 MiB heap, 256 KiB stack). No modules, filesystem, network, clock, or random source. Initialization has no observation. One call per 1/60 s simulated tick; physics waits for the result. The external watchdog charges elapsed decision time including handoff, at most 20 ms per step and 100 ms per aligned 20-step cycle. Submit with margin. An overrun discards the action, terminates the interpreter, and waits out the current cycle. The next cycle restarts your code. Invalid actions and thrown exceptions wait. After a successful dispatch no commands are required during transfer.

`o={t,stage,yaw,ride,manuals,rays}`. Clock, current stage (1..10), head orientation, transfer latch and the original handbooks carried by the party are personal information. The other agents join at stages 3 and 7. Current amendments, panic and sanity of another actor can be inspected by looking at them. No global entities, room map, true hazard results, or evidence counter is provided. `rays` is null between scans. At `t%20==0`, it has 27 slots: elevation −15°, 0°, +15°; within each elevation yaw −60°, −45°, …, +60°. Positive yaw is clockwise from −Z, +X is right. Origin is the captain's eye, 1.45 m up. Range is 12 m. The nearest scored solid blocks the ray, including a solid in shadow. A miss or unlit hit returns null. Each readable hit has `kind,at,size,point,lit` (metres; size is full XYZ extent, at is center) plus subtype fields. Marks, text and the outer appearance of an object are decorative.

## Actions

| Code | Operation |
|---:|---|
|0|Wait.|
|1 / 2|Turn eyes +60° / −60°. Next optical scan uses new heading.|
|3|Reassure the party on a beat boundary. Reduce each panic by five.|
|4|Dispatch party simultaneously on currently reserved carriages. An unfilled reservation or handbook refusal keeps everyone at the console.|
|100+10×actor+id|Reserve or replace a witnessed carriage for actor (captain=0). Temporal guard checked at release; all other guards checked on booking.|
|200+10×actor+id|Show a colleague evidence for a room-local amendment. The referee checks actual observations in **this room**: saw that same machine extended and retracted, and its socket accepts this actor. Accepting adds an alternative to the intact native handbook (specific carriage ID, matching socket, hold ≥40 ticks). It replaces a prior local amendment at zero panic cost.|
|300+10×actor+id|Unsupported order, companions only. Reserve a witnessed carriage despite the handbook, temporarily bypass its release check, add 42 panic (cap 100).|

Bookings and proofs always refer to the local id of a carriage actually seen. Orders have no action-success oracle. Waiting and looking are safe at the console while calm. At every beat a panicked actor loses `ceil(panic/14)` sanity, and with probability `panic/100` abandons their booking and returns to the native manual. At sanity ≤55 another draw with that probability can interrupt the next teammate's reservation and silently narrow the actor's effective rule (hold ≥100). Sanity 0 ends the run. This stochastic process is seeded. Mental state persists between rooms; local rule references and reservations expire. The captain issues orders; each companion accepts/refuses by evaluating its current AST.

## Physical instrument sheet

- All phases switch on the scan lattice. `phase=(floor(t/20)+offset)%period`. Exposed gears use periods dividing 120 beats. Score geometry consists of axis-aligned envelopes, apart from marked drum fixtures (upright regular 12-gons; the Dawn Bell top radius is .64 of its base radius). `physics.mjs` is the public mathematical implementation for AST evaluation, time windows and optical intersections.
- `carriage`: `id`, `seals` (handbook certification bit mask), `sockets` (shoe geometry bit mask), `bank` (shared power winding), signed lever `arm`, `period,width,offset,phase,stroke,hold,assigned`. Stroke is extended for `phase<width`, retracted otherwise. `hold=max(0,(width-phase)*20-t%20)` is time to disengagement. The lift locks after 20 ticks of support. Height is 2 m extended / 1.2 m retracted. Its local read lamp is blacked out for `phase==period-1`; the entire face is unreadable then. Each other scored surface has steady local light.
- `governor`: its plate publishes a local executable `limit` AST, gear `mode,period,offset,travel,phase`. Roles weigh 1,2,3 units; their shoes have bit 0,1,2 respectively. For a group, physical `peak` is the largest number loading one winding. `torque` is sum of weight × carriage arm plus gear bias: zero at phase zero, otherwise `(phase%3-1)*travel`. `clearance=floor(period/2)-phase`. `contact` is the XOR of `1<<bank` for every occupied position (one term per actor). Group interlocks engage when multiple actors dispatch. Read the calibrated plate in each room; do not assume the same permissible loads. A single carriage has one passenger position. Transfer rails carry the party through the cleared whitebox to the next console in 200 ticks.
- `crew`: includes role, current reservation `id`, native `manual`, currently remembered `rules`, `forced,panic,sanity`. Architecture hits: `wall,floor,apron,fixture`; fixtures carry `shape,label`. Surfaces are opaque. The scored geometry and lamp states determine readings, independent of spectator camera and rendering quality.
- AST nodes are prefix arrays: `all` / `any` have child predicates; `bit(field,i)`, `eq`, `ge`, `le` operate on a named scalar and a literal. Native manuals are conservative solo operating envelopes. Local physical coupling applies to a party releasing together. An instrument can be physically compatible outside the native certification. The handbook, instrument laws and measurements are the evidence available to the program.
