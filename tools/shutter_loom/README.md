# A04 · Shutter Loom model / view contract

Two identical silhouettes lie beyond an iron rail. From the porch, throw the counterweight towards either side. The slab rides that way, cutting the window ray on that side and uncovering the opposite walking mouth. Watch the dark receiver: the genuine silhouette disappears with its ray; the fixed ink silhouette stays in place. If the tested silhouette stays dark, take the opposite mouth now. If it vanishes, let the wall return, throw to the other side, and cross the now-open genuine mouth. The same slab creates both the optical experiment and the walking route. The ink receiver catches anyone who continues through its mouth after the ink ridge rises; `back` from the safe approach aborts the attempt.

```text
                      EXIT                 +Z
          west receiver | east receiver     gallery
              ↗                      ↖      window rays, plan view
          west mouth ==== rail ==== east mouth
          west approach | east approach
                   porch + lever
          west slit              east slit   −Z
             −X         0         +X
```

A throw runs one nominal 720-tick cycle and returns to the lever-ready home detent. There is a dwell from tick +140 to +640. A crossing already reserved by +620 can finish during the return warning: a mechanical interlock holds the slab in its berth until that crossing resolves or is cancelled. Both initial beams and both shadow patches look identical on either seed parity. Use equal initial art; the surviving mark in the cut beam is the diagnostic. The mirror layouts change ink placement only. A02 and the other rooms are outside this model; A04 uses only 2C + 5B.

## API

Preload `res://scenes/physics_lab/shutter_loom/ShutterRules.gd`; construct with `Rules.new(seed)` (default `0`) or call `reset(seed)` on an existing instance.

| Call | Contract |
|---|---|
| `layout()` | Static plan, seed-independent. Plan coordinates are integer `[x,z]` cm; divide by 100 for Godot metres. `bounds_cm` is `[min_x,min_z,max_x,max_z]`; `partition_x_spans_cm` are the three solid spans at `partition_z_cm`, between and outside the mouths. The high window rays pass above the fixed partition; only the tall moving slab intersects them. Place the slab at `[rail.wall_x_cm, layout.rail_z_cm]` using `wall_half_extents_cm`. Each `lanes[side]` entry gives its mouth, approach, window, ray midpoint, uncut endpoint, caster, receiver and shadow polygon. The west polygon mirrors the east winding. |
| `snapshot()` | Fresh dictionary, safe to hold/mutate. `tick`, `tick_hz`, `next_decision_tick`, and `outcome` (`active`, `won`, `caught`). `player` gives `zone`, `lane` (`""` when outside or on porch), and `position_cm`. `rail` gives `phase`, `wall_x_cm`, and `target_lane` (the latched berth side until the cycle ends). Each `lanes[side]` gives `beam_reaches_receiver`, `beam_tip_cm` (first slab contact or full endpoint), `shadow_visible`, `mouth_clear`, and `crossing_available`. Use `mouth_clear` for geometry and `crossing_available` for the detent permit. `cues` lists live physical warnings/reservations; `events` contains the current tick's event pulse. |
| `submit(action)` | One request on each tick divisible by 20, including tick 0. Returns `{accepted, reason}`. Rejections on an open boundary consume that slot. This may append a cue to the current tick's existing `events`; read after submitting before advancing. Action order and cadence are independent of render speed. |
| `tick_once()` | Advances one model tick, refreshes `events`, and runs the active timers. Use a fixed 60 Hz driver. Observe each tick to preserve transient events; render interpolation can use adjacent `position_cm` and `wall_x_cm`. The model clock and rail continue through terminal outcomes; transit actions are locked. |
| `step(action = "wait")` | Replay helper: submit, then advance 20 ticks. Returns `{accepted, reason, events, snapshot}`. The ordered `events` tape contains just the events added by that action and those generated by the ensuing ticks. Off-boundary or already-used requests advance zero ticks. |

**Actions:** `wait`, `throw_west`, `throw_east`, `approach_west`, `approach_east`, `back`, `cross`, `exit`. The paired throws are in reach from the porch and approaches and require the home detent. The approaches and `back` move within the safe forecourt. At the selected approach, `cross` reserves the open berthed passage. It starts the actual walk after a 60-tick lineup and reaches the receiver at +100. `back` can cancel during lineup. `exit` becomes available after genuine arrival; reaching the exit takes 40 ticks. No debug actions are exposed. Optional engine collision/navigation should respect the two mouths, use the supplied crossing permit, and route progression through this API.

**Rail phases:** `home → outbound_warning → outbound → berthed → return_warning → [held] → returning → home`. The slab starts moving after the +60 steam mark; the ray and walking aperture switch on geometric contact at separate ticks during the stroke. Show the actual beam endpoint and use `shadow_visible` to draw the same receiver patch every time.

| Cue kind | Physical view anchor and treatment |
|---|---|
| `rail_steam_out`, `rail_steam_home` | Rail-side nozzle at `position_cm`, with steam or a chain tug pointing along the stroke. Return warning stays live through a held departure; `rail_interlock` is the clamp engaging. |
| `threshold_scuff` | Footfall or edge scuff at the approach, followed by the walk. |
| `ink_ridge` | Raised ink at the far receiver, appearing +20 after the cross request; there are 40 ticks to retreat before walk begins and 80 before the catch. |
| `exit_lintel` | Latch/door movement at the exit as the player walks towards it. |

Live cues: `{id, kind, lane, start_tick, due_tick, position_cm}`. Events use `{type, tick, kind, lane, cue_id}`, with `due_tick` and `position_cm` on `cue` and `lead_ticks` on `commit`. A cue's `due_tick` marks the first scheduled action it warns about; the same warning can cover later commits (including a delayed return). `note` covers cancellation/interlock without claiming a new spatial transition. `mouth_open/close`, `beam_cut/restore`, `rail_depart_*/dock_*`, `walk_begin/arrive`, `ink_snap`, and `exit_arrive` are commit events.

## Headless replay

```powershell
& .runtime/godot/4.7.2/Godot_v4.7.2-stable_mono_win64_console.exe --headless --path . --script res://tools/shutter_loom/verify.gd
```

`golden.json` is A04-only: run-length encoded decisions, sampled states (lane bits are beam/shadow/mouth), exact event rows `[tick,kind,lane,cue_id,due_tick_or_lead]`, and terminal outcome. The verifier compares per-tick and batched playback, independently ray-tests the finite slab, audits the cue tape, and checks mirrored placement, standby, blocked mid-stroke crossing, withdrawal, and the delayed-return catch. This fixture is for machine verification; the layout and snapshot API above are sufficient to build the view.
