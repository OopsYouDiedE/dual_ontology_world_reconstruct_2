# A07 Strata — view interface

The carriage holds one reversible **Copper / Ceramic** core. Copper closes the traction rail and accepts charge from the anchor's harness spring. Ceramic breaks the circuit and fits the narrow stairs. Carry the core to the crown and power its hatch with the same bank that powered ascent.

Preload `res://scenes/physics_lab/strata/StrataRules.gd`. `terrain()` gives the fixed band geometry and physical station names. `initial()` gives a fresh model state. `snapshot(state)` gives a detached view dictionary. `legal_actions(state)` gives the accepted action IDs. `step(state, action)` returns a new state after one 20-tick decision; an invalid action leaves the state and clock unchanged. Render the returned `snapshot`, never advance the model to take a screenshot. `advance(state, action, trace)` is the same decision, optionally appending all 20 single-tick snapshots to the supplied array; use this for animation or verification. Tick rate is 60 Hz. No mutable resources or scene nodes are required.

## Stable view fields

- `tick`, `landing` (0–3, last fully reached), `height_cm`, `route` (`anchor`, `rail`, `stairs`), `form` (`copper`, `ceramic`), `mode` (`ready`, `thermal_press`, `hatch`, `return`, `won`). `target_form` is empty outside the press. Routes are two alignments in a single vertical section; terrain gives each band's height.
- `strain`, `strain_warn`, `strain_hard`, `charge`, `charge_max`, `press_cost`, `hatch_cost`, `boost_cost_next`, `unload_rate`, `strain_per_charge`, `circuit` (`open`, `rail`, `regenerative`, `hatch`). Display exact readings alongside gauge fill; `strain_hard` is the relief-valve cap, not an immediate failure threshold. `strain_warn` starts the sustained-load timer.
- `warnings`: array of `{id, started_tick, due_tick, ticks_left}`. IDs: `strain_trip`, `form_change`, `hatch_open`, `return_ready`. Clear a warning if it disappears; play its resolved effect from `events`, not from clamping a local view timer. `events`: `{id, tick}` for events on this snapshot; decision snapshots aggregate all events within the decision. Treat them as a receipt for the view's most recent input, not a queue to replay on passive inspection. Snapshot `actions` mirrors `legal_actions`.
- `terrain()` returns `landings`: `[{id, name, height_cm}, ...]`, and `bands`: `[{from_cm, to_cm, rail_hand_cm_per_tick, rail_boost_cm_per_tick, stairs_cm_per_tick, rail_hand_strain_per_tick, rail_boost_strain_per_tick, stairs_strain_per_tick, rail_boost_draw_per_tick}, ...]`. `height_cm` is absolute and rises within an attempt; the cradle returns to the Sump in one state transition. Lane alignment is derived from `route`. Boost cost includes only ticks still needed to reach the next anchor. `unload_rate` is the nominal strain shed per tick at an anchor.
- Actions: `climb` (hand crank on Copper rail or walk in Ceramic stair), `boost` (Copper powered climb), `unload` (at anchor), `transform` (at anchor), `open_hatch` (crown), `wait` (including during a timed operation). They are lower-case IDs. Landing arrival clamps motion; spare ticks in a climb decision idle at the anchor.

Boot/reset uses `initial()`. Freeze/pause can be view-side by issuing no decisions. Expose identical action semantics to player and test driver. Do not derive availability from a timer or a visual rail collision; use `actions`.

## Cue anchors and state transitions

- `strain_trip`: Amber at the harness and its gauge from strain 420 upward. The first decision-boundary receipt always has 41–60 ticks remaining. The gauge can reach its relief cap (660) without ending the attempt early. If an anchor unload brings strain below 420 by the deadline tick, the cue clears. Otherwise `strain_tripped` engages the automatic cradle. `return_ready` then warns for another 60 ticks before `returned` restores the Sump, Copper core and initial gauges; the global tick is preserved.
- `form_change`: 24 charge is committed immediately, `form_started` opens the contact, and `target_form` previews the other detent. `form_changed` flips the route selector at the indicated deadline. Preserve stored strain through the flip. `wait` is the only accepted action during the press.
- `hatch_open`: 130 charge is committed on `hatch_started`; `hatch_opened` changes `mode` to `won`. A closed Copper contact and strain below 420 are required to start. The whole action remains a 20-tick slot even if commitment occurs before its boundary.
- Live interpolation may use the trace array; only ticks divisible by 20 accept new decisions. At the end of each decision, replace the cues and controls with the final snapshot. Other event IDs: `rail_entered`, `stairs_entered`, `landed`, `strain_warning`, `strain_cleared`.
